The problem

The barrier is not eligibility. It is discovery.

15% of Indian adults access formal credit against a 24% global average. 89% hold a bank account, and 16% of those accounts sit inactive. Just 14% of 63 million MSMEs — employing 90% of the workforce — have formal credit access, against a ₹25 lakh crore gap.

Most of the people on the wrong side of those numbers already qualify for something. What stands between them and it is a discovery process that assumes literacy, English, and a smartphone. Setu removes all three assumptions: you speak one sentence, in your language, and the system does the rest — over a browser today, over a missed call in the design for the next stage.

The architectural commitment

Everything else in this document follows from one decision

The model's job is narrow: turn speech into structured JSON, and turn the engine's output into a plain spoken sentence. It never decides eligibility.

That constraint is not caution for its own sake. Four properties this system needs are only available on the far side of it:

  • No hallucination surface on the verdict. The decision is Python over typed predicates. There is no temperature on it, no prompt to regress, and no version of the model that changes who qualifies.
  • The Ladder can exist at all. Counterfactual search requires rules you can invert one at a time and re-evaluate. You can invert a predicate; you cannot invert a judgement.
  • Every answer is citable, because each rule carries the government sentence it was transcribed from.
  • Marginal cost stays near zero. One ASR pass and one extraction per turn. Everything downstream is deterministic Python, so a thousand more schemes cost nothing per query.

Three-valued logic

A missing fact is never a failure

A two-valued engine has to answer no when it doesn't know. That single default is the behaviour this product exists to replace, so the verdict type has three members rather than two.

✓ELIGIBLE

Every gating rule passed on facts we actually hold.

✕NOT_ELIGIBLE

A gating rule failed on a fact the caller stated. This is the only path to a "no".

?NEED_INFO

Nothing failed, but a fact we need is missing. It is a question to ask, not a rejection.

Status is carried by an icon and a word as well as a colour, here and in the app, so the distinction survives a monochrome screen or a colour-blind reader.

The distinction runs all the way down to individual documents. Holding no record of a bank account and being told "I don't have one" are different states, so the profile carries two lists — documents and documents_denied — and a document rule returns three values against them. Subsumption is explicit too: a Jan Dhan account satisfies any rule asking for a bank account, because it is one.

Both the ASR and LLM layers sit behind single-function adapters, so replacing a provider is one function body, not a rewrite. That matters more than it sounds: the model in use today was picked by measurement three days ago and the previous choice had already been withdrawn for new API keys.

The model layer

Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite on the free tier. Extraction runs at temperature=0 against a responseSchema, so the JSON reaching the engine is structurally guaranteed rather than hoped for. eval/pick_model.py timed the candidates against the same prompt: this one matched the accuracy of larger models at 1.3s against 10.3s.

The single most important line in the extraction prompt instructs the model to leave unstated fields null. A guessed age becomes a wrong verdict the person never gets to contest, so silence must never be allowed to become a fact.

Data model

The catalogue is the product; the code is the machinery

Adding a scheme means adding JSON, not writing Python. Every rule carries four things beyond its predicate, and tests/test_rules.py validates all four on every rule — so a typo fails the test suite rather than a live demo.

     directly to that account.",

  // how a failure gets fixed — the Ladder can only offer what is here
  "remedy": {
    "action": "Open a zero-balance Jan Dhan account using your Aadhaar",
    "where":  "Any public sector bank branch or Bank Mitra",
    "cost_rupees": 0,
    "days": 3,
    "grants": { "field": "documents", "value": "jan_dhan_account" }
  },

  // remedies that must be completed first
  "depends_on": ["svanidhi_has_aadhaar"]
}

13 of the 39 rules carry a remedy; 26 carry null. Age and income ceilings deliberately have none. You cannot act your way out of being 43, and a ladder that pretends otherwise sends someone to an office to be turned away — a day of lost wages and a bus fare, spent on our optimism.

Derived fields

People answer in the units they think in, which are rarely the units a scheme is written in. Three fields are computed rather than asked: monthly_income_est from daily earnings at 26 working days, annual_turnover, and vending_since_year_est from age and the age they started working — because almost nobody remembers the calendar year they began, and everybody remembers being fifteen. That inference is arithmetic in the engine, never a model guess.

The Ladder

Not qualifying is a position, not a verdict

When a scheme fails on remediable rules, Setu searches the counterfactual space: invert each failing rule via its remedy, re-evaluate, and keep the routes that land on ELIGIBLE. Paths are ordered by dependency, costed in rupees and days, and grouped by their first step — because one action usually unlocks several schemes at once, and the caller needs one thing to do tomorrow morning, not five.

Live engine output

Street vendor, 34, Maharashtra · ₹400/day · holds Aadhaar · no bank account
  1. 01
    Open a zero-balance Jan Dhan account using your Aadhaar
    Any public sector bank branch or Bank Mitra ₹0 3 days unlocks ₹5,46,000 across 5 schemes

Five schemes — PMJJBY, PMSBY, Atal Pension, MUDRA Shishu and PM-SYM — were each NOT_ELIGIBLE on the same rule. The search collapses them into one errand. The rung is ordered after Aadhaar via depends_on, because you cannot open the account without it.

Every path is replayed before it is shown

verify_path() walks the ladder on a copy of the profile and confirms it genuinely lands on ELIGIBLE. Paths that don't are discarded rather than displayed. A route that doesn't actually work is worse than admitting there is none, because the person spends real days finding that out.

A consequence worth stating

Strict verification means a scheme disappears from the Ladder while any of its rules is still NEED_INFO — the replay can't prove a route lands on eligible when a fact is unknown. In the run above, PM SVANidhi is absent for exactly this reason: the vending-proof rule hasn't been answered yet.

This is the correct behaviour and not a gap being papered over. The missing fact is surfaced to the narrator as documents:vending_certificate, so the conversation asks about it, and the path appears once it's answered. The alternative — showing a route under an unverified assumption — is the failure mode the verification exists to prevent.

Auditability

Every verdict points at the sentence it came from

Tapping "Why?" shows the document, the URL, and the quoted government sentence behind each rule that fired. Users and auditors verify decisions instead of trusting them — and since the engine is a lookup over those rules, the citation is the actual cause of the verdict, not a justification assembled afterwards.

An earlier version of the catalogue cited page numbers. They were not verifiable against the published documents, so they were replaced with URLs and quotes checked against the source. Doing that check turned up four real eligibility rules that had been missing from the catalogue entirely — which is the argument for citations in one line: the discipline of having to cite is what caught the errors.

Language

Three offered, eight supported

The app offers Hindi, Marathi and English. Whisper auto-detects the spoken language; edge-tts answers in it with a neural voice, at no cost and with no API key.

The voice layer carries five more — Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Gujarati — and the pipeline is language-agnostic throughout. The picker is deliberately shorter than the capability: those five have not been tested end to end with real speakers, and a language that half-works in front of a user is worse than one that isn't offered. They are there for the IVR stage, where the missed-call funnel needs them.

Scheme cards translate; citations keep their original

A Hindi conversation that hands back an English scheme card has solved nothing, so benefit text, rule descriptions and clause quotes are all translated — one batched, cached call per response rather than one per string.

The government's exact published sentence is kept alongside the translation and labelled as the original, rather than replaced by it. A translated clause reads better, but it is no longer what the government wrote, and a citation quietly paraphrased has stopped being a citation.

A perfect score here is the expected result, not a boast. The decision layer is a lookup over typed predicates; it has almost no error surface. What this eval actually proves is that the catalogue was transcribed correctly from the source documents — which is where the real risk lives. It is a test of the data, not a claim about a model.

The number worth arguing about is the second one

Precision and recall are measured on complete profiles. False rejections on partial information are measured on people who have said one sentence — which is the state they are actually in when the system first has to behave well.

The two failure modes cost different things. A false positive sends someone to a government office to be turned away: a day of wages and a bus fare. A false negative silently withholds money they were entitled to, and they never find out. The second is what this product exists to fix, which is why NEED_INFO is a first-class verdict rather than a rounding error — and it is measured separately for the same reason.

This eval caught a real bug: missing_fields() had been inspecting only NEED_INFO results, so an empty document list was reaching the engine as a failure rather than an unknown. Every partial persona was being rejected for facts nobody had asked them for.

Both caches key on a content hash, so a repeated sentence costs nothing and returns instantly. Scheme-card translation is one batched call per response and per language, not one per string — the whole 80-string catalogue warms in a single call each for Hindi and Marathi.

Adding schemes does not add cost. The extraction prompt doesn't grow with the catalogue and the matching is local, so a hundred more schemes are still two model calls per turn.

Operational design

What breaks in a live demo, and what was built for it

Conference wifi

SETU_LLM_MODE=offline and SETU_VOICE_MODE=offline make both adapters refuse network calls and serve only from cache. scripts/prewarm.py walks whole conversations through the live pipeline, translates the full catalogue into each language, and then re-runs everything with the network refused to verify the cache actually covers it. A warm cache that hasn't been checked in offline mode is a cache you're guessing about.

"Is it the mic, the model, or the rules?"

POST /ask/text runs the identical pipeline from typed input — no microphone, no HTTPS tunnel, no Whisper. When something breaks twenty minutes before a demo, that endpoint isolates the layer in one curl.

Free-tier rate limits

15 requests per minute. Every call carries a three-attempt retry with linear backoff, because a judge running the demo twice in a row is precisely the burst that would otherwise fail in front of them.

getUserMedia refuses plain HTTP

The browser will not grant microphone access outside a secure context, which localhost satisfies and a phone on the same LAN does not. The phone demo therefore runs behind ngrok http 8000. Audio playback is triggered by an explicit user gesture rather than autoplay — Chrome not only blocks autoplay without one, it declines to load the file at all, leaving a silent player with duration: NaN.

Roadmap

Designed and specified, not present in this repository

Listed here because the architecture accommodates them — not because they ship today.

Missed-call callback — the zero-cost funnel

The channel that reaches the users who need Setu most: no smartphone, no data plan, no app, no literacy. The user gives a missed call. Setu hangs up, calls back at its own cost, and runs the same flow over IVR — language on the keypad, spoken question, spoken answer carrying the match and the next step. It costs the user nothing, which is the whole point: every rupee of friction at the top of the funnel removes exactly the people the scheme was written for.

What it needs and does not have here: a Twilio webhook to catch the inbound call and trigger the callback, a keypad language menu, and an audio bridge between TTS output and the call leg. The loop it sits on top of — ASR, extraction, rules, Ladder, TTS — is built and tested, and the five extra voices are already in place for it.

Others

Retrieval over the full scheme corpus for free-form questions beyond the rule set; Document Doctor, catching name mismatches before an application fails silently; Voice Ledger, producing a bank-readable cash-flow statement from spoken daily takings.
